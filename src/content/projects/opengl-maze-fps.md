## Overview

A C++ first-person shooter with procedural mazes, core engine systems, and visibility-based rendering.

## Implementation

- Independently developed a 3D first-person shooter featuring procedurally generated mazes, textured geometry, dual camera modes, and animated skyboxes driven by Perlin noise and Diamond–Square fractals.
- Implemented core engine systems including player physics, shooting mechanics, enemy AI behaviors, and collision detection.
- Improved rendering performance using tile-based visibility raycasting to cull walls, enemies, and projectiles outside the player’s field of view.
