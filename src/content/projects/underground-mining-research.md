## Research context

Selected as 1 of 10 students (120+ applicants) for an NSF-funded Research Experience for Undergraduates (REU) in Computer Science.

Undergraduate Researcher at Missouri University of Science & Technology, June–August 2025.

## Contributions

- Co-developed a simulation environment modeling underground miners, wireless sensors, and battery depletion.
- Trained reinforcement learning agents (PPO, PPO-LSTM) using CNN and attention-based architectures.
- Implemented a predictive D* Lite variant in C++ with PyBind11 bindings.
- Built a custom PyGame renderer to visualize agent learning and route optimization.
