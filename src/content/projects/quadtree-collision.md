## Overview

A particle simulation comparing quadtree-based and brute-force collision detection with FPS benchmarking.

## Implementation

- Developed a 2D particle simulation comparing quadtree-based and brute-force collision detection for 1000+ dynamically colliding particles.
- Integrated FPS benchmarking to empirically evaluate performance tradeoffs between collision methods.
